Privacy Policy – V-Music Player

Effective Date: 5 August 2026

V-Music Player ("the App") is developed by Vishvesh Lad. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles user information.

Information We Collect

V-Music Player is designed to work completely offline and does not collect, store, or share any personal information such as name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, videos, messages, or payment information.

Music Files Access

The App may request permission to access audio files stored on your device in order to display your music library, play songs, create playlists, and mark songs as favorites. These files are processed only on your device and are never uploaded to any server.

Internet Usage

V-Music Player does not require an internet connection for normal functionality. The App does not send your music files or usage data to the developer or any third party.

Advertisements

V-Music Player does not display advertisements.

Analytics and Tracking

The App does not use analytics, tracking tools, or third-party advertising SDKs.

Data Sharing

No user data is sold, shared, rented, or transferred to third parties.

Security

All music playback and related processing occur locally on your device. No cloud storage or remote servers are used by the App.

Children’s Privacy

V-Music Player is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future. Any changes will be reflected by updating the Effective Date above.
