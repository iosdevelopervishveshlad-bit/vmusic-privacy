SECTION 01

Information We Collect

V-Music Player (“the App”), developed by Vishvesh Lad, is designed to work completely offline and does not collect, store, or share personal information.

SECTION 02

Music Files Access & Sharing

The App requests permission to access audio files stored on your device so it can display your music library, play songs, create playlists, and mark songs as favorites. These files are processed locally on your device.

If you choose to use the App’s Share feature, the App passes the selected local media file to the Android System Share Sheet. The App itself does not upload, copy, or transmit your media to developer servers.

Data may be transferred to third-party apps, such as messaging apps or email clients, that you explicitly select from the Android Share menu. Those apps handle shared content according to their own privacy practices.

You decide whether to share a music file and which app receives it.
SECTION 03

Internet Usage

The App does not require an internet connection for normal functionality and does not send your music files or usage data to third parties.

SECTION 04

Advertisements

V-Music Player does not display advertisements.

SECTION 05

Analytics and Tracking

The App does not use analytics, tracking tools, or third-party advertising SDKs.

SECTION 06

Data Sharing

No user data is sold, shared, rented, or transferred to third parties by the App. This does not include a file you deliberately share with another app using Android’s Share Sheet.

SECTION 07

Security

Music playback and related processing occur locally on your device. The App does not use cloud storage or remote servers for these functions.

SECTION 08

Children’s Privacy

V-Music Player is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

SECTION 09

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future. Any changes will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this page.
